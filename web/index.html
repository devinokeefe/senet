      Rules (Modern Kendall, 5 pieces)
      • Throw 4 sticks: light sides up = 1–4, none = 5. Throws 1, 4, 5 earn another throw.
      • Move one piece forward exactly the throw. Land on a lone enemy to swap places.
      • Two friendly pieces side by side protect each other; three in a row cannot be passed.
      • No forward move? You must move backward. No move at all? The turn passes, even after a 1, 4 or 5.
      • Every piece must stop exactly on 26 (Beauty). Landing on 27 (Water) sends the piece back to 15 — or, if 15 is taken, to the highest empty square below it.
      • Bear off from 26 with a 5, from 28 with 3, 29 with 2, 30 with 1; pieces on 28–30 cannot move otherwise. First to bear off all five wins.
